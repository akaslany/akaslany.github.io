Privacy Policy — N2Y Personal Publisher

Last updated: 20 October 2026

N2Y Personal Publisher ("the app") is a private, single-operator automation operated by the owner of the YouTube channel it publishes to. It has no third-party users and no public sign-up. This policy describes the only data the app handles.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Google account authorization data. An OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token for the channel owner's own Google account, limited to the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl.
  • YouTube channel and video metadata for the owner's own channel. The app reads the list of videos already uploaded to the owner's channel (to avoid duplicate uploads) and reads back a video's metadata after upload to verify it.
  • Content the owner supplies. The research source, the generated report, the rendered video file, title, description, tags and thumbnail.

2. How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose: uploading the owner's finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel and verifying that upload. The app does not profile users, does not build advertising profiles, and does not use the data for any other purpose.

3. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in a local file on the owner's own server, readable only by the owner's operating-system account. Generated reports and video files are stored locally until the owner deletes them. The app keeps no server-side database of YouTube user data. Revoking the app's access in the Google Account permissions page (or deleting the local token file) ends all access immediately.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, transfer or disclose any data to third parties. Data leaves the owner's server only as the upload request sent to the YouTube Data API on the owner's behalf.

5. Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking of any kind.

6. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and has no end users other than the channel owner.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
