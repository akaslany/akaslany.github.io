N2Y Personal Publisher

N2Y Personal Publisher is a single-operator automation that publishes the channel owner's own research explainer videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not a public service and has no third-party end users: the only account that authorizes it is the channel owner.

What it does

  • Takes a research source the owner supplies (a paper, an article, or a video) and produces a Korean explainer report and video.
  • Uploads that finished video, with its title, description, tags and thumbnail, to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads back the uploaded video's metadata to confirm the upload matches what was produced.

Authorization

The app requests the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl, which is used only for uploading and reading back the owner's own videos. Credentials are stored on the owner's own server and are never sent anywhere else.
